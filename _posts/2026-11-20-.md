Hyungkwon Ahn and Donguk Kim, under the supervision of Professor Soondo Hong, won an Excellence Award at the 2026 Logistics Paper Competition hosted by the Korean Society of Logistics Science and Technology.

Their paper, entitled “A Rescheduling Framework for Incident Recovery on Single-Track Railway Sections under Different Levels of Traffic Control Intervention,” was honored for its scholarly merit and potential application to railway operations.
